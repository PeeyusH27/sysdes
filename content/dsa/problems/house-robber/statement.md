A street has a row of houses, and `nums[i]` is the amount of cash kept in house `i`. Neighbouring houses share an alarm: if two **adjacent** houses are both robbed on the same night, it goes off.

Return the largest total amount of cash you can take without ever robbing two adjacent houses. You may skip as many houses as you like, including several in a row.

### Constraints

- `1 <= nums.length <= 100`
- `0 <= nums[i] <= 400`
