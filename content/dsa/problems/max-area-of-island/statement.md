You are given an `m x n` grid of numbers, where `1` is land and `0` is water. An **island** is a group of `1`s joined horizontally or vertically (not diagonally), and its **area** is the number of cells in it.

Return the area of the largest island in the grid, or `0` if the grid has no land.

### Constraints

- `1 <= m, n <= 50`
- `grid[r][c]` is `0` or `1`
