A staircase has `n` steps, and `cost[i]` is the price you pay each time you **step on** step `i`. After paying for a step you may climb **one or two** steps up. You may begin on step `0` or on step `1`. The top of the staircase is the position just past the last step (index `n`), and it costs nothing to arrive there.

Return the minimum total cost to reach the top.

### Constraints

- `2 <= cost.length <= 1000`
- `0 <= cost[i] <= 999`
