You are given a list of meetings in any order, where `intervals[i] = [start, end]`. Each meeting needs a room from time `start` until time `end`. A room that is freed at time `t` can be used straight away by a meeting starting at time `t`.

Return the **minimum number of rooms** needed so that every meeting has a room for its whole duration.

### Constraints

- `0 <= intervals.length <= 10^4`
- `0 <= start < end <= 10^6`
