Houses stand around a **circular** street, and `nums[i]` is the money inside house `i`. You may rob any set of houses as long as you never rob two houses that are next to each other. Because the street is a circle, the first house and the last house are neighbours.

Return the largest total you can rob. If there is only one house, you may rob it.

### Constraints

- `1 <= nums.length <= 100`
- `0 <= nums[i] <= 1000`
