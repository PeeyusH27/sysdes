You are given the heads of two linked lists, `list1` and `list2`, each sorted in **non-decreasing** order.

Merge them into one sorted list by **splicing their nodes together**, without creating a new node for each value, and return the head of the merged list. Either list may be empty.

### Constraints

- Each list has between `0` and `50` nodes.
- `-100 <= Node.val <= 100`
- Both lists are sorted in non-decreasing order.
