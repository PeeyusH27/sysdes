You are given an array `height` of `n` non-negative integers. Each value is the height of a vertical line drawn at that index on the x-axis.

Choose two lines that, together with the x-axis, form a container. Return the **maximum amount of water** a container can hold. Water can't be slanted: its level is the height of the **shorter** of the two lines, and its width is the distance between their indices.

### Constraints

- `2 <= height.length <= 10^5`
- `0 <= height[i] <= 10^4`
