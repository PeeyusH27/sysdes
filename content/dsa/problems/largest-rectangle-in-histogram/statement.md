You are given an array `heights` describing a histogram: bar `i` has width `1` and height `heights[i]`, and the bars stand side by side with no gaps.

Return the area of the **largest rectangle** that fits entirely inside the histogram. The rectangle is axis-aligned and may span several adjacent bars, but it can't be taller than the shortest bar it covers.

### Constraints

- `1 <= heights.length <= 10^5`
- `0 <= heights[i] <= 10^4`
