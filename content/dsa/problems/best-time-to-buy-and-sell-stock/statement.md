You are given an array `prices`, where `prices[i]` is the price of a stock on day `i`.

You may make **at most one** trade: buy one share on some day and sell it on a **later** day. Return the largest profit you can make. If no trade makes money, return `0`: you simply don't trade.

### Constraints

- `1 <= prices.length <= 10^5`
- `0 <= prices[i] <= 10^4`
