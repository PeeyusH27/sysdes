You are given `n` non-negative integers `height` representing an elevation map where each bar has width `1`. Compute how much water it can trap after it rains.

Water above a bar is held in place only if there is a taller (or equal) bar somewhere to its left **and** somewhere to its right.

### Constraints

- `1 <= height.length <= 2 * 10^4`
- `0 <= height[i] <= 10^5`
