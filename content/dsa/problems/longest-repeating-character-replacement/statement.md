You are given a string `s` of uppercase English letters and an integer `k`.

You may perform **at most `k` operations**. Each operation changes one character of `s` into any other uppercase letter.

Return the length of the longest substring that consists of a **single repeated letter** after your operations.

### Constraints

- `1 <= s.length <= 10^5`
- `s` contains uppercase English letters only.
- `0 <= k <= s.length`
