You are given the `head` of a singly linked list and two integers `left` and `right` with `left <= right`. Positions are **1-based**: the head is at position 1.

Reverse the nodes from position `left` to position `right`, inclusive, leaving every other node where it is, and return the head of the list. Rewire the nodes rather than swapping their values.

### Constraints

- The list has `n` nodes, `1 <= n <= 500`.
- `-500 <= Node.val <= 500`
- `1 <= left <= right <= n`
